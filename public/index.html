about us

A family dairy farm in Coimbatore

Sree Sivasakthi Dairy Farm has been supplying fresh milk and dairy products straight from our own farm in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We milk, chill, and prepare everything ourselves — no distributors, no middlemen — so what reaches your doorstep is as close to farm-fresh as it gets.

Every batch of curd, butter and ghee is made the traditional way, in small quantities, so quality never gets diluted for scale.
